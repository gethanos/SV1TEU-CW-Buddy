About SV1TEU CW Buddy

SV1TEU CW Buddy is an online browser-based Morse Code (CW) training and QSO assistant for amateur radio operators. Users can generate realistic CW QSOs, supporting prosigns, play Morse audio at adjustable speeds, and decode CW using a microphone. Ideal for ham radio practice, CW learning, contests, and ragchews. Try the live demo in your browser without installation.
